MRI Prior Authorization Policy

Procedure Codes: CPT 70551, 70552, 70553 (MRI brain without and with contrast).

Prior authorization is required for all outpatient MRI procedures unless emergency criteria are met.

Medical Necessity Requirements:
- Patient must have completed at least 6 weeks of conservative treatment including physical therapy or medication management.
- Clinical indication must be documented in the medical record.
- Referring physician specialty must be appropriate for the requested imaging.

Exclusions:
- MRI is not covered for screening purposes without documented high-risk factors.
- Experimental or investigational uses are excluded.

Emergency Exception:
- Emergency cases with documented acute neurological deficit may bypass conservative treatment requirements.

Approval Criteria:
- When all medical necessity requirements are met, the prior authorization request should be APPROVED.
- Deny when conservative treatment documentation is incomplete or procedure is not medically necessary.
