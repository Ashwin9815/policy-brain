# 8. UX Architecture

## Purpose
Policy Brain prioritizes guided workflows, transparency and explainability over traditional document editing.

## UX Principles
- AI assists, users control.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Explain every AI decision.
- Keep users in context.
- Minimize cognitive load.

## Primary Navigation
- Dashboard
- Knowledge
- Policies
- Workflows
- Search
- Administration

## Rule Generation
The system asks clarifying questions one at a time, while allowing users to provide free-form answers at any stage.

## Collaboration
Users can add comments as questions, suggestions, issues, requests, approval notes or evidence, with private or shared visibility and @mentions.
