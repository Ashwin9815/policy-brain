# 2. Product Philosophy

## Purpose
Policy Brain is designed as an enterprise platform for governed policy authoring, not a generic AI chatbot.

## Core Principles

1. AI proposes; humans approve.
2. Knowledge is the canonical source of truth.
3. Explainability is mandatory.
4. Governance overrides automation.
5. Every decision is traceable.
6. Every workflow is replayable.
7. APIs are first-class citizens.
8. Multi-tenant isolation is non-negotiable.

## Product Values

- Enterprise-first
- AI-native
- API-first
- Modular architecture
- Replaceable infrastructure
- Secure by design
- Observable by default
- Built for extensibility

## Long-term Vision

Policy Brain evolves from an AI policy compiler into a complete Enterprise Policy Operating System capable of managing organizational knowledge, governance, and executable business rules.
