# 1. Vision

## Purpose

Policy Brain is an enterprise AI platform for transforming unstructured policy documents into governed, explainable and executable business rules.

## Vision

Enable organizations to convert institutional knowledge into trusted digital policy assets while ensuring:

- AI proposes, humans approve.
- Every decision is evidence-backed.
- Every policy is versioned.
- Every workflow is replayable.
- Every action is auditable.

## Guiding Principles

- Knowledge is the source of truth.
- Explainability is mandatory.
- Governance overrides automation.
- APIs are first-class.
- Enterprise-ready from day one.
