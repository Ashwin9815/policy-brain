# 5. User Personas

## Purpose
This section defines the primary users of Policy Brain and the outcomes they expect from the platform.

## Primary Personas

### Product Manager
- Authors and reviews policy logic
- Drives product changes
- Collaborates with engineering and operations

### Policy Analyst
- Creates and validates business rules
- Reviews AI-generated outputs
- Maintains policy accuracy

### Medical Director
- Reviews clinical correctness
- Approves production policies
- Ensures regulatory compliance

### Compliance Officer
- Audits changes
- Reviews decision traces
- Verifies governance requirements

### Platform Administrator
- Manages users, folders and permissions
- Configures integrations and AI settings
- Monitors system health and usage

## Shared Goals
- Faster policy authoring
- Explainable AI outputs
- Secure collaboration
- Complete auditability
- Reduced manual effort
