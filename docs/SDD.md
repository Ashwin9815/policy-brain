# Software Design Document (SDD)

## AI Policy Compiler & Enterprise Knowledge Platform

**Version:** 0.1 (Living Document)

This document is the single source of truth for the product.

## Vision
Build an enterprise AI Policy Compiler that transforms healthcare policies, documents and natural language into auditable, versioned, explainable decision rules while keeping humans in control.

## Core Principles
- AI proposes, humans approve.
- Knowledge is the source of truth.
- API-first architecture.
- Event-driven workflows.
- Tenant isolation.
- Evidence-based decision traces.
- Multi-agent orchestration.
- Checkpoint-based recovery.
- BYOK + managed AI support.
